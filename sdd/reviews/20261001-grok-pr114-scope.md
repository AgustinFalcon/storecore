## Persist wires

`PaymentStatus` is a Kotlin `sealed class`. Known cases match the payments CHECK. `fromWire` trims and maps anything else to `Unknown`, whose wire is `unknown`. `isApproved()` is true only for `Approved`. `forUnpaidTermination` maps `CommercialEffect.REJECT` to `Rejected` and every other effect, including `CANCEL` and `UNKNOWN`, to `Cancelled`. That matches the previous `REJECT` versus else-`CANCELLED` branch.

Checkout accreditation is `PaymentStatus.fromWire(order.paymentStatus).isApproved()`. Stored payment rows are exact CHECK tokens, so trim does not accredit a new legal status.

Worker writes that changed:

- payments accredited: `PaymentStatus.Approved.wire` → `APPROVED`
- orders accredited: `OrderStatus.Paid.wire` / `PaidStockReview.wire` → `PAID` / `PAID_STOCK_REVIEW`
- payments unpaid: `Rejected.wire` or `Cancelled.wire`, guarded by `Pending.wire` → `REJECTED` or `CANCELLED` where status is `PENDING`
- orders unpaid: `OrderStatus.Cancelled.wire` where `PendingPayment.wire` → `CANCELLED` where `PENDING_PAYMENT`
- application transition: `PAYMENT_${paymentStatus.wire}` → `PAYMENT_REJECTED` or `PAYMENT_CANCELLED`

`reverse` does not update `payments.status`. Attempt states `ACCREDITED` and `TERMINAL_UNPAID_VERIFIED`, inbox processing statuses, and provider transaction columns stay literal and are outside this cut. `PaymentStatusTest` covers the closed set, trim, `Unknown` without echoing the raw wire, and reject versus cancel.
