# PCC-001 — notification domain evidence

Date: 2026-11-22

## Delivered boundary

- Closed notification severity, category, source, lifecycle and command-step vocabularies.
- Neutral `UNKNOWN` translation for future wire values; unknown alerts remain readable and cannot execute commands.
- Step objects own the exact acknowledge, resolve and reopen transition graph.
- Immutable alert aggregate with checked revisions and no synthetic replay.
- Typed source, episode, condition, actor and command identities with the SDD bounds.
- Versioned SHA-256 fingerprint and command-payload hash using unsigned 32-bit big-endian UTF-8 length framing.

Persistence, durable receipts/replay, authorization, HTTP, email and UI remain outside PCC-001.
