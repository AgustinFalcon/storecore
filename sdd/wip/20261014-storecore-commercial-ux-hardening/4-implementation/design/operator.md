## R6 composition decision — coordinated Astra consensus

The modern-UI and product/UX reviewers jointly approved a stricter generation strategy: exactly two immutable artifacts per remaining surface. Artifact A is the usable Demo operational baseline with one target, one authorized role and one current state. Artifact B is a companion of complete, isolated state scenes with target, role, fixture and applicability declared outside the product frame. B is design evidence, not an additional application route.

- `SC-SURF-20A/B`: twelve-order baseline; filters, selection and supported fulfillment in A. CSV, confirmations, result/error/Unknown states and reduced Real contract in B.
- `SC-SURF-21A/B`: pristine ORD-1015 detail in A. Five-step return, inventory disposition, partial refund, idempotency, logistics and exact Real RMA chain in B.
- `SC-SURF-22A/B`: five exact balances, reservations and contextual commands in A. Editable adjustment/reconciliation, conflict/history and read-only Real inventory in B.
- `SC-SURF-23A/B`: synchronization baseline and five correlated variants in A. Full Account, Publications, Activity and Competition compositions, mapping, retry and target cuts in B.
- `SC-SURF-24A/B`: seven capability facts and authorized navigation/change entry points in A. CATALOG command lifecycle, reconciliation, all closed states and role/target variants in B.
- `SC-SURF-25A/B`: five Demo settings and collapsed import entry in A. Reset/export plus isolated Demo and Real import workflows with exact schema/diff states in B.
- `SC-SURF-26A/B`: Admin customer list and canonical customer-2209 in A. restriction/reactivation/session effects, full states and read-only/denied roles in B; no Real variant.

Common constraints: canonical SC15 shell and routes; no new capability, fixture, route or business JavaScript; semantic static controls only; no before/after specimen mutates the shared baseline; every artifact must independently reflow at 390, 768 and 1440 without lost actions or fixed-width mobile rails. Angular remains blocked until both artifacts for all seven surfaces pass both coordinated Astra gates.
